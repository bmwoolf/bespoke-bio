## Coding variant evidence
- **SERPINA1 S**: precursor E288V, mature E264V, rs17580. [ClinVar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/RCV000177031.61/) cites gnomAD frequency 0.023354 (Feb 2017 dataset).
- **SERPINA1 Z**: precursor E366K, mature E342K, rs28929474. [ClinVar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/RCV000148877.26/) cites ExAC 1.8%; population frequencies depend on ancestry. UniProt links Z to A237, whereas canonical is V237: the supplied sequence is an E366K-only substitution, not an asserted phased natural Z haplotype.
- **FGB**: precursor R478K, conventional signal-cleaved R448K, rs4220; our isolated domain coordinate R247K. A [2025 primary population study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11829138/) reports gnomAD MAF 0.170. Sequence/residue and dbSNP identity independently confirmed in UniProt P02675.

LEP uses model 1 of the deposited 8K6Z NMR ensemble, selected before outcomes; no best-model selection or full ensemble claim.
